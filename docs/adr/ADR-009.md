### 1. Context & Motivation

Phase 8 formalizes the mechanical stop functional $M_{\text{stop}}(x(t))$ over a deterministic ingress-to-brake timing budget:

$$\Delta t_{\max} = \tau_{\text{override}} + \tau_{\text{fsync}} = 11.99\text{ ms} + 1.20\text{ ms} = 13.19\text{ ms}$$

Adversarial inspection of the Phase 8 specification verified that software denial ($D(a) = \text{DENY}$) cannot halt continuous momentum ($\dot{x}(t_0) \neq 0$), confirming the mathematical validity of the double-integrator stopping envelope. However, an analysis of `proofs/Projection.v` identified that the `noise_bound` record field in `ContinuousObservation` remains unconstrained in the inductive invariant proofs.

Under asynchronous telemetry dropouts or bounded sensor drift ($\Vert{}d(t)\Vert{} \le d_{\max}$, $\varepsilon =$ `noise_bound`), a pointwise evaluation of $h(\hat{x}) - M_{\text{stop}}(\hat{x}) \ge 0$ could pass on a noisy observation $\hat{x}$ while the true continuous state $x(t)$ breaches the envelope $h(x(t)) \ge 0$ during the latency window $\Delta t_{\max}$.

---

### 2. Decision: The Robust Gronwall Margin ($\gamma$)

We close the continuous-to-discrete observation gap as an internal refinement to Phase 8 without introducing continuous ODE solvers, floating-point calculations, or runtime memory allocation into the Layer-0 or Phase 8 kernels.

1. **Continuous-Time Invariance Model:**  
   For a control-affine plant $\dot{x} = f(x) + g(x)u + d(t)$ with local Lipschitz constants $\Vert{}f(x) - f(y)\Vert{} \le L_f \Vert{}x - y\Vert{}$ and $\Vert{}g(x) - g(y)\Vert{} \le L_g \Vert{}x - y\Vert{}$, we define the composite Lipschitz constant:
   $$L = L_f + L_g \Vert{}u\Vert{}_{\infty}$$

2. **The Offline Robust Certificate:**  
   Applying the Bellman-Gronwall inequality over the worst-case operational window $\Delta t_{\max} \le 13.19\text{ ms}$, we derive the exact robust reachability margin $\gamma$:
   $$\gamma = \varepsilon e^{L \Delta t_{\max}} + \frac{d_{\max}}{L}\left(e^{L \Delta t_{\max}} - 1\right)$$

3. **Discrete Integer Witness Injection:**  
   The continuous differential inequality is mechanized strictly offline (via Coq/Coquelicot or Lean). The offline proof extracts a verified fixed-point or rational constant $\gamma \in \mathbb{Q}$.
